VERIFIABLE ZERO-TELEMETRY PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy Policy — Minimum Phone

Developer: kasb LLC
Application ID: com.kasbllc.minimumphone
Effective Date: October 18, 2026

1. Zero Data Collection & Zero Internet Permission

Minimum Phone is built with a strict 100% offline-first architecture. The Android application does not request the android.permission.INTERNET permission. Therefore, it is technically impossible for Minimum Phone to transmit, upload, sell, or share any personal data, screen-time metrics, journal entries, purity streaks, or notification contents to external servers.

2. How On-Device Permissions Are Used

  • Default Home Launcher (android.intent.category.HOME): Used solely to provide the minimalist monochrome text-only home screen and app drawer on your device.
  • Usage Stats (android.permission.PACKAGE_USAGE_STATS): Used strictly locally on your device to calculate your daily screen time and trigger mindful breathing delays when opening user-selected restricted apps.
  • Notification Policy & Digest: Used locally on your device to batch non-urgent notifications into your scheduled digest times and enable grayscale focus effects.
  • NFC / Camera (Optional): Used locally to scan your physical QR/NFC focus anchor card to unlock strict focus sessions.

3. Data Storage & Deletion

All app settings, custom notes, journal reflections, and streak counters are stored exclusively in your device's local storage. Uninstalling the application or clearing app storage permanently deletes all stored data immediately.

4. Third-Party SDKs, Ads, and Analytics

Minimum Phone contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, zero cloud analytics trackers, and zero paywalls.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact kasb LLC via our official support email listed on our Google Play Store developer page.
